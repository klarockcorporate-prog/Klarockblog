What the parks include

Nextipac II, Circuito Metropolitano I and Periférico Oriente II offer 24/7 security, an access control gate, a perimeter wall with electric fence, hydraulic-concrete internal roads and a double fire network under NFPA standards.

Published rent runs from MXN 120.00 to MXN 140.00 per m² of built area per month, plus VAT. Maintenance is billed separately and includes common-area cleaning, lighting, 24-hour private security and waste collection. All units are available for immediate delivery.

Why Guadalajara

Zapopan and Tonalá are part of the Guadalajara metropolitan area, one of Mexico's main industrial and technology centers. Small and mid-size buildings suit light manufacturing, assembly, storage and distribution.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to lease an industrial building in Guadalajara?

In Klarock's Zapopan and Tonalá inventory, published rents range from MXN 120.00 to MXN 140.00 per square meter of built area per month, plus VAT. For example, Building A01 at Nextipac II (987.64 m²) leases for MXN 118,516.80 per month + VAT. Maintenance is billed separately.

What building sizes are available?

From 576.69 m² built (Building A01 at Periférico Oriente II) to 1,976.29 m² (Buildings B18 and B19 at Nextipac II), on lots of 635.87 to 2,219.05 m².

What does park maintenance include?

Common-area cleaning, amenity reservations, common-area lighting, internet within the amenities, 24-hour private security and waste collection for offices and common areas.

When can I take occupancy?

All 11 units are available for immediate delivery. Confirm availability at the time of your inquiry with a managing partner.

Can I lease several adjacent buildings?

Yes. Published combinations include A01 and A02 and D02 and D03 at Periférico Oriente II, and B18 and B19 at Nextipac II. Contact us if you need more space.
