Nave Industrial Clase AAA — Ciénega de Flores, Nuevo León

Class AAA industrial park with 67,220 m² of leasable area in two buildings and 4 combinable modules, available for immediate operation in Ciénega de Flores, Nuevo León, on the Zuazua highway.

Two buildings (A: 32,120 m², B: 35,100 m²) split into 4 combinable modules, with central warehouses from 2,200 m² and front warehouses of 500–600 m². Located on the Zuazua highway, in the industrial corridor north of the Monterrey metropolitan area. Ideal for light manufacturing, logistics and distribution.
