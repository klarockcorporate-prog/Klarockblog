General specifications

All buildings have a 15 cm (6 in) floor, sprinkler system (shared in some buildings), 20–30 FC LED lighting (T5 in Building 14) and 3–4 air changes per hour in most.

They suit manufacturing, maquila and export logistics. The table shows size, clear height, docks, parking spaces and CTPAT readiness for each building.

Frequently asked questions

What building sizes are available at San Ramón Industrial Park?

Eight buildings from 1,255 m² (Building 5) to 17,856 m² (Building 12), totaling 41,349 m².

What clear height do the buildings have?

Between 4.85 and 11.02 m, depending on the building. Building 15 reaches 11.02 m and Building 12 reaches 10.79 m.

What does CTPAT Ready mean?

CTPAT is a U.S. Customs and Border Protection supply chain security program. “CTPAT Ready” indicates the building is prepared to operate under that program; confirm requirements with your customs broker.

Are the buildings for lease or for sale?

They are available for lease.
