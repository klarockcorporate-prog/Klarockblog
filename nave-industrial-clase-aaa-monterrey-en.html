Suited operations

The project is designed for light manufacturing, logistics and distribution. Modules are combinable, so you can lease anything from 500–600 m² front bays to full modules as your operation grows.

Frequently asked questions

Can I lease only part of the project?

Yes. The project is divided into 4 combinable modules, with central bays from 2,200 m² and front bays of 500 to 600 m².

What clear height and floor capacity does it have?

Clear height ranges from 11 to 14 m, with a 15 cm slab rated at 4 tons per m².

What is the electrical capacity?

2,400 KW with an expansion option, a CFE substation at the front and a 900 KW clean-energy project.

Where is it located?

On the Zuazua highway in Ciénega de Flores, in the industrial corridor north of the Monterrey metropolitan area.
