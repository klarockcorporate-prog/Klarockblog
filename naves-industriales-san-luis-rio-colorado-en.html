Specifications

19,880 m² building: second-generation facility ready for immediate delivery, with an FM Global-approved sprinkler system, 12 loading docks, 314 parking spaces, electrical transformers and water tanks included in the lease.

INPARK Building 1 (16,964 m²): 12 loading docks, 191 parking spaces, 32 ft clear height, a 500 KVA electrical transformer, water tanks and a 400-ton air conditioning system included.

Suited operations

The listings describe them as ideal for light manufacturing, medical devices, electronics and metal-mechanic manufacturing, in an area known for its skilled and stable workforce.
